## C0 High-level system description
A reconstructed university education-management system used as an experimental monolith. It is based on documented SEMS functional areas, not original production source.

### Functional areas (documented capabilities, not a labeled assignment of classes)
- faculty: faculty and department administration; staff and teacher records; course catalog administration; academic calendar; faculty-level statistics
- student: student registration and profile; semester registration; course enrollment; student academic overview
- grade: exam scheduling; exam registration; grade recording and updates; grade history; GPA calculation
- finance: tuition and semester fees; student invoices and payments; account balances; financial transactions; faculty income and expense ledger
- attendance: class session management; student attendance; staff attendance; monthly attendance reports
- library: book catalog and copies; library membership; loans and returns; reservations

### Known cross-domain dependencies (narrative)
- Grade workflows require student, teacher, course, and often enrollment records.
- Attendance workflows require student, teacher, course, and enrollment records.
- Finance workflows require student and faculty context for invoicing and ledgers.
- Library membership is issued to students and staff.
- Student information is read by Student, Grade, Attendance, Finance, and Library.
- Course ownership sits with Faculty administration but is consumed by Student, Grade, and Attendance.
